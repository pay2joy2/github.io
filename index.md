# Privacy Policy — Us: Couple Mood & Cycle Sync

Last updated: May 2026

## What data we collect
- Google account email and display name (for authentication)
- Daily mood and wellness check-in data
- Menstrual cycle data (optional, only if provided by user)
- Partner connection data (invite codes)

## How we use data
- To provide the core app functionality
- To sync mood data between paired partners
- We do NOT sell or share your data with third parties

## Data storage
- Data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore)
- Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication
- All data transmission is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)

## Data sharing between partners
- You control what data your partner can see through Privacy Settings
- You can disconnect from your partner at any time
- Disconnecting removes mutual access to each other's data

## Data deletion
- You can delete your account by contacting us
- Upon deletion, all your data will be permanently removed

## Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.
If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to do necessary actions.
